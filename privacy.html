Privacy

Privacy

Plain language: what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Updated November 3, 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, email address, the books and role you choose, and any message. We use it to send the sample or news you asked for, or to answer your message.

Email

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Service providers

Signups and messages are stored with the email or form service the publisher uses for that purpose. If site analytics are switched on, they count visits and clicks in aggregate (for example, which pages are read and which buttons are used); they do not identify you by name.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, reply to any email from us or use the form on the contact page.

Last updated 2026-11-03.
