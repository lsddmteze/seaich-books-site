Subject description

A specialized theological interpretation, not a general introduction. Seaich develops his interpretation of grace, works, salvation and exaltation through the Bible, the Book of Mormon, the Dead Sea Scrolls, the Nag Hammadi texts and the early Church Fathers, arguing that distinctive Latter-day Saint teachings were widely known in the Primitive Church. Independent scholarship; not an official publication of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
