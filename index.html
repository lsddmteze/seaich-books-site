The author

Eugene Seaich (1925–2006)

Seaich earned five degrees, among them a Ph.D. in Music from the University of Utah (1958). He was a Fulbright grantee in Germany in 1955–56, a registered pharmacist, and taught at the University of Utah. He wrote on the Dead Sea Scrolls, the Nag Hammadi library, the early Church Fathers and the temple.
