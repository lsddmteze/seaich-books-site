Key concepts

What the book means by its central terms — definitions in Seaich’s sense first, then the 2026 annotation note.

Updated November 3, 2026

Mescaline

The phenethylamine alkaloid of the peyote cactus — the substance of Seaich’s first and sixth sessions, and of Huxley’s Doors of Perception.

Definition →

LSD

Lysergic acid diethylamide — the substance of seven of the nine sessions, investigated philosophically rather than clinically.

Definition →

Set and setting

Mindset and environment — the variables Seaich attends to across his sessions, before the phrase became standard.

Definition →

Phenomenology

The philosophical method of the book: describing experience as it presents itself, before explaining it away.
