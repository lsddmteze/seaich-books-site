Context

A short history of psychedelics

Where The Far-Off Land sits in the long story of sacred plants, laboratory chemistry, prohibition and today’s research.

  1. Pre-ColonialIndigenous use of peyote, psilocybin mushrooms, and other entheogens across the Americas for ritual, healing, and divination.
  2. 16th c.Spanish chroniclers document Aztec sacramental use of Teonanácatl and other psychoactive plants.
  3. 1897Arthur Heffter isolates mescaline from peyote through deliberate self-experimentation.
  4. 1938Albert Hofmann first synthesizes LSD-25 at Sandoz Laboratories, Basel, Switzerland (November 16).
  5. 1943Hofmann discovers LSD’s psychedelic properties on April 19, “Bicycle Day.”
  6. 1954Aldous Huxley publishes The Doors of Perception, the first major literary account of the mescaline experience.
  7. 1957Humphry Osmond coins the term “psychedelic.” R. Gordon Wasson publishes his mushroom experiences in Life magazine.
  8. 1958–59Hofmann isolates psilocybin and psilocin from Psilocybe mexicana. Sandoz distributes LSD to researchers worldwide.
  9. 1959John Eugene Seaich writes The Far-Off Land and begins his self-experiments with mescaline and LSD in Salt Lake City, Utah.
  10. 1960October 22: the ninth and final journal entry.
  11. 1966–70Legal suppression of psychedelic research; the Controlled Substances Act (1970) classifies LSD and psilocybin as Schedule I.
  12. 1994American Indian Religious Freedom Act Amendments protect peyote use in the Native American Church.
  13. 2000sClinical trials at Johns Hopkins, Imperial College London, NYU and elsewhere revive scientific interest in psychedelics for depression, addiction, PTSD and end-of-life distress.
  14. 2006John Eugene Seaich dies. His grandson finds the typescript in his library.
  15. 2012A first, self-published edition of The Far-Off Land appears.
  16. 2017The FDA grants Breakthrough Therapy designation to MDMA for PTSD.
  17. 2018The FDA grants Breakthrough Therapy designation to psilocybin for treatment-resistant depression (COMPASS Pathways). Breakthrough designation is not approval.
  18. 2019The FDA grants Breakthrough Therapy designation to psilocybin for major depressive disorder (Usona Institute).
  19. 2024The FDA issues a complete response letter declining to approve MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD.
  20. 2026The Complete Annotated Edition is published. No classic psychedelic or MDMA therapy has yet been approved by the FDA.
