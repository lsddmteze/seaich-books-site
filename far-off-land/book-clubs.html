Book clubs

A group read with a difference: a philosopher’s firsthand account from 1959, read against everything that came after.

Updated November 3, 2026

Starting questions for your group

  1. Seaich wrote before the 1960s culture wars. How does that change the way his questions land compared with later psychedelic writing?
  2. He treats the experience as a philosophical problem rather than a medical or spiritual one. What does that framing open up — and what does it leave out?
  3. The edition restores the 1959 text and labels every 2026 addition. Did the separation change how you read either voice?
  4. Which of the nine journals (I–IX) stayed with you, and why?
  5. How do Seaich’s questions compare with what current research (COMP360, the May 2026 Nature Communications study) is asking?
  6. After the final chapter: did the book change your mind about anything?
