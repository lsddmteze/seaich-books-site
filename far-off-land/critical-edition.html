The Far-Off Land · Editorial method

The critical edition

Written in 1959, kept for decades as a typescript, issued in a rough self-published edition in 2012, and now restored and annotated. Here is how the 2026 text was made.

Updated November 3, 2026

How the text was treated

Seaich’s prose is reproduced as he wrote it. nothing of substance, argument or style is altered, modernized or abridged. His chapter structure and numbering (I to XI) are kept. Where a word or reference needs explaining, the explanation goes in the editorial apparatus, never into his sentences.

Restored from the 1959 typescript

A collation against scans of the original typescript returned a number of passages and words to the text, including:

  • The Introduction’s sentence on the general application of private experiments (“Nor does the private nature of my experiments…”)
  • The full D. H. Lawrence passage from New Mexico in Chapter V, where earlier versions had a paraphrase
  • The Chapter VIII passage beginning “Doubt destroys our capacities to experience beauty”
  • The author’s own English rendering of the Aeschylus fragment in Chapter XI
  • The ninth and final journal (October 22, 1960)
  • The typescript’s epigraphs, title-page verse and the journals’ title page
  • The author’s wording in dozens of places where it had been silently altered (for example “which” for “that”)

The apparatus

Three kinds of annotation accompany the text: marginal symbols where Seaich’s description anticipates a later finding, numbered endnotes with sources and context, and set-apart commentary boxes that connect a passage to current research. The back matter adds a glossary, an annotated bibliography, a biographical index, a guide to research organizations, a documented record of the author’s training, a parallel chronology, a topical guide and facsimile pages.

What the journals are, and are not

The nine journals are reproduced as historical documents. They belong to the legal and scientific context of 1959–1960 and are neither medical advice nor a guide to be followed.
