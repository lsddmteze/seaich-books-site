Volume 6: Gnosticism and the Book of Abraham

The Nag Hammadi Texts, the Papyri, and the Facsimiles

The closing volume takes on the hardest problems: Gnostic literature from Nag Hammadi, the Joseph Smith papyri, and the Book of Abraham facsimiles — testing Latter-day Saint claims against the ancient sources with the series’ full scholarly apparatus.

About this volume

Part of Ancient Texts and Mormonism: A Historical and Theological Study of Primitive Christianity, Early Apocrypha, Patristic Writings, and Restored Latter-day Saint Doctrine (2026 Annotated Edition), Eugene Seaich’s study of ancient texts and Latter-day Saint theology, revised and edited with a foreword by Eric Hendrickson. Print length: 485 pages.
