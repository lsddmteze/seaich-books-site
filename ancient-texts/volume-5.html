Volume 5: The Temple

Ancient Temple Symbolism, the Jerusalem Temple, and the Modern Temple

Temple theology across the ancient world: the symbolism of the Jerusalem temple, its rituals and architecture, and their relationship to the modern Latter-day Saint temple. Builds on Seaich’s 1981 SEHA paper on cherubim and temple symbolism and his Gorgias Press volume A Great Mystery: The Secret of the Jerusalem Temple.

By Eugene Seaich · Revised and edited, with a foreword, by Eric Hendrickson

About this volume

Part of Ancient Texts and Mormonism: A Historical and Theological Study of Primitive Christianity, Early Apocrypha, Patristic Writings, and Restored Latter-day Saint Doctrine (2026 Annotated Edition), Eugene Seaich’s study of ancient texts and Latter-day Saint theology, revised and edited with a foreword by Eric Hendrickson. Print length: 828 pages.

Publication schedule and prices are announced as each volume clears proof.
