The short author bio (verified — reuse everywhere)

Eugene Seaich (1925–2006) was a scholar of ancient religious texts, a musicologist, and a Fulbright scholar. He earned five degrees, including a Ph.D. in Music from the University of Utah (1958), and wrote A Great Mystery (Gorgias Press) and the six-volume Ancient Texts and Mormonism.

# Seaich Legacy Publications — the writings of Eugene Seaich (1925–2006)

> Annotated editions of Eugene Seaich, edited by his grandson Eric Hendrickson: The Far-Off Land (written 1959; Complete Annotated Edition 2026), a philosophical essay and nine dated LSD and mescaline journals; Understanding Mormonism (2026 Annotated Edition), on grace, salvation and Latter-day Saint theology; and Ancient Texts and Mormonism (2026 Annotated Edition, six volumes).

## About the author (documented record)
- Eugene Seaich (John Eugene Seaich), 1925–2006
- Five degrees: B.M. (Composition), McCune School of Music and Art, 1948; B.F.A. with High Honors, University of Utah, 1951; M.F.A., University of Utah, 1952; Ph.D. in Music, University of Utah, 1958; pharmacy degree, University of Utah College of Pharmacy
- Fulbright grantee in Germany, October 1955 – July 1956 (University of Munich); Phi Kappa Phi (1951); Lehn & Fink Gold Medal (1965); registered pharmacist (Utah, 1967); taught at the University of Utah
- Other works include A Great Mystery: The Secret of the Jerusalem Temple and Mormonism, the Dead Sea Scrolls, and the Nag Hammadi Texts

## The Far-Off Land
- The Far-Off Land: LSD, Mescaline, and a Lost 1959 Classic of Psychedelic Research, Memory, and Consciousness — The Complete Annotated Edition, 2026; 260 pages; paperback and Kindle
- Written 1959 in Salt Lake City, when LSD and mescaline were legal in the United States; first, rough self-published edition 2012; text in the restored edition in 2026
- Eleven essay chapters and nine journals (August 1959 – October 22, 1960; two mescaline, seven LSD)
